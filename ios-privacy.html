IOS PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

This policy describes the free iPhone version of Sleep Tracker AI 1.0.7. The iOS app has no sign-in screen, subscription, In-App Purchase, external checkout, or advertising tracking.

Information stored on your iPhone

Sleep sessions, alarm settings, estimated sleep phases and scores, detected sound events, app preferences, onboarding answers, dream journal entries, and sleep audio are stored locally to provide the features you request. Recording starts only after you start a sleep session and allow microphone access.

Optional AI reports

Before the first network AI report, the app asks for permission. If you allow it, aggregated sleep metrics—such as dates, intervals, duration, estimated scores and phases, awakenings, event summaries, selected activities, goals, timezone, and locale—may be sent through Sleep Tracker AI infrastructure hosted on Supabase to Anthropic for report generation.

Raw sleep audio, dream journal text, your name, email, contacts, and precise location are not sent for AI reports. You may decline or withdraw AI consent without disabling local sleep tracking.

Diagnostics and analytics

The app may process pseudonymous user or device identifiers, app version, operating-system version, feature interactions, crash reports, and performance diagnostics through Supabase, Sentry, and PostHog to operate the service, prevent abuse, understand reliability, and fix errors. The app does not use this information to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your iPhone until you delete it in the app or remove the app. A pseudonymous server identity is created only when a network AI feature requires it. Profile → Data & Privacy provides controls for AI consent and deletion. You may also request deletion by contacting support.

Health disclaimer

Sleep Tracker AI is an informational wellness app, not a medical device. Estimates and reports are not diagnoses or medical advice. The app does not use HealthKit or CareKit.

Contact

Кратко на русском

Версия Sleep Tracker AI 1.0.7 для iPhone не содержит входа в аккаунт, встроенных покупок, внешней оплаты и рекламного трекинга. Данные сна и аудиозаписи хранятся локально. Перед сетевым AI-отчётом приложение отдельно запрашивает согласие на передачу агрегированных показателей сна. Аудиозаписи, текст дневника снов, имя, email, контакты и точная геопозиция для AI-отчёта не отправляются. Согласие можно отозвать, а локальные и серверные данные — удалить через раздел Data & Privacy или запрос в поддержку.
